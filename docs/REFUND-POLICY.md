# Refund Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to paid offerings by `epikodelabs` in connection with `actionstack` and `streamix`.

## 1. Open Source Access

Access to the public `actionstack` repository, package, and documentation is provided at no charge under the GNU Affero General Public License v3. Because there is no purchase required for open-source access, no refund applies to that access.

## 2. Commercial Licenses

Commercial licenses for `actionstack` and `streamix` are delivered digitally and grant perpetual rights. Because of this, **all license purchases are final and non-refundable** unless otherwise required by law.

Exceptions may be considered at our discretion:

- ✅ Refund requests may be considered within 7 days if the license has not been used or was mis-delivered.
- ✅ If a technical defect prevents activation, we offer a replacement or refund at our discretion.
- ❌ No refunds for change of mind or discontinued projects.

## 3. Paid Services

If you purchase paid support, consulting, training, or custom development services, refund eligibility is governed by the applicable written agreement, quote, invoice, or statement of work.

Unless a separate written agreement states otherwise:

- Fees for work already performed are non-refundable
- Completed milestones, delivered reviews, and completed support sessions are non-refundable
- Prepaid amounts for work not yet performed may be refundable on a pro rata basis
- Third-party expenses, taxes, and non-recoverable payment processor fees are non-refundable once incurred

## 4. Cancellations

If you need to cancel a paid engagement, contact `epikodelabs` as soon as possible. Cancellation requests are evaluated based on the remaining scope, committed scheduling, and any costs already incurred.

## 5. Exceptions

If `epikodelabs` cannot deliver a paid service substantially as agreed and the issue is not cured within a reasonable time, a partial or full refund may be issued at `epikodelabs`' discretion or as required by the governing written agreement.

## 6. Contact

Refund requests should be sent to `oleksii.shepel@outlook.com` with enough detail to identify the engagement and payment. For license refunds, include the invoice number.
