If you purchase paid support, consulting, training, or custom development services, refund eligibility is governed by the applicable written agreement, quote, invoice, or statement of work.

Unless a separate written agreement states otherwise:

- Fees for work already performed are non-refundable

## 3. Cancellations

If you need to cancel a paid engagement, contact `epikodelabs` as soon as possible. Cancellation requests are evaluated based on the remaining scope, committed scheduling, and any costs already incurred.

If `epikodelabs` cannot deliver a paid service substantially as agreed and the issue is not cured within a reasonable time, a partial or full refund may be issued at `epikodelabs`' discretion or as required by the governing written agreement.
